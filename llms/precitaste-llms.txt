# PreciTaste

> PreciTaste is the AI kitchen management platform for restaurants. It forecasts demand and tells kitchen crews what to prep and cook — when, and how much — reducing food waste by up to 50% and helping operators run every location like their best one. Deployed across 5,000+ locations globally.

PreciTaste (formerly PreciBake) was founded by Ingo & Laura Stork-Wersborg, with offices in New York City, Munich, and India. Its AI agents identify, track, and assess kitchen task processes — demand forecasting, prep and production planning, predictive ordering, and real-time food tracking — for QSR, fast-casual, full-service, c-store, bakery, and other foodservice operations. ## Products & Tools
- [All Solutions and Tools](https://precitaste.com/allsolutions/): Overview of the full PreciTaste suite of AI kitchen-management modules.
- [Daily Prep Management](https://precitaste.com/daily-prep-management/): AI-guided daily prep planning that tells crews what to prepare and how much.
- [Hourly Production Planning](https://precitaste.com/hourly-production-planning/): Real-time, hour-by-hour cook-and-hold guidance based on predicted demand.
- [Predictive Ordering](https://precitaste.com/predictive-ordering/): Automated inventory and supplier ordering driven by demand forecasts.
- [Do'Cast](https://precitaste.com/docast/): PreciTaste's demand forecasting engine.
- [Demand Forecasting](https://precitaste.com/demand-forecasting/): AI demand prediction from sales, weather, events, and other signals.
- [Recipe Viewer](https://precitaste.com/recipeviewer/): Digital recipe and prep instructions at the station.
- [Checklist](https://precitaste.com/checklist/): Digital task and food-safety checklists for kitchen teams.
- [Label Printer](https://precitaste.com/label-printer/): Automated prep and date labeling.
- [Real-Time Food Tracking](https://precitaste.com/real-time-food-tracking/): Computer-vision tracking of food levels on the line.
- [Data Analytics](https://precitaste.com/data-analytics/): Operational analytics and reporting across locations.
